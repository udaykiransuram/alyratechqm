Planned Scope Under This Direction

Public Flow Components `app/(public)/summer-crash-course/*`, `InnerHero`, public CTA/card sections, and the summer registration/sign-in/help clients.
Signed-In Summer Components `app/(student)/student/crash-course/*`, payment card treatment, diagnostic-submitted presentation, and summer-specific surface styling.
Shared Tokens / CSS Update the public summer palette and the summer-specific student styles in `app/globals.css` so the design is systemic, not page-local.
Not Changing No behavior changes, no auth flow logic changes, no student portal shell replacement, and no deep content rewrite.
