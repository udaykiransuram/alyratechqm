Section 1 · Visual System

Warm Clinical Premium

The summer-crash flow should feel more trustworthy, more expensive, and far less generic, but still easy for families to use on a phone. The visual system leans on deep teal, clean white surfaces, softer academic neutrals, stronger CTA contrast, and tighter mobile spacing. It should feel like a serious education product with warmth, not like a default admin panel.

Trust first Every screen should feel stable, clear, and credible for parents under mild stress.
Mobile clarity Shorter section stacks, stronger CTA grouping, and cleaner scan order on small screens.
One family of surfaces Landing, sign-in, register, report, and student home should feel related instead of separate micro-products.

Palette Direction

Keep teal as the emotional anchor, but make it deeper and more controlled. Reduce the “bright marketing teal” feel, add warmer neutrals, and create more premium surface separation.

Primary Ink Teal hero shells, premium emphasis, high-trust sections
Action Teal primary buttons, active states, conversion moments
Mint Surface soft page glow, cards behind utility content
Warm Neutral subtle human warmth for parent-facing copy blocks
Deep Ink headline typography, important numerics, report emphasis
Support Tint meta pills, progress chips, gentle success surfaces

Typography & Rhythm

Use the current type stack more intentionally: sharper title spacing, calmer body text, and less oversized marketing treatment. The feeling should shift from “generic promo page” to “considered premium product”.

Repair weak maths foundations with a calmer, clearer mobile flow.
Headlines stay compact and serious. Body copy becomes shorter, more scannable, and less airy on phones.
Labels, helper text, meta chips, and summary strips should all feel part of the same cadence: restrained uppercase where useful, stronger numerics, and fewer decorative type moments.

Mobile UI Treatment

This is the intended feel for register, sign-in, help, and student-home shells on mobile: high-trust header, tighter content stack, more premium form/card treatment, and cleaner CTA grouping.
